Data & Account Deletion

At LittleSpark, we value your privacy. This page explains how your data is handled and allows you to request data deletion.

Did you know? LittleSpark does not use or create online user accounts. All of your child's game progress is saved locally on your device. You can completely delete all data instantly by uninstalling the app or clearing the app cache in your device's settings.
